---
name: S.N.O.W. staff decisions
description: Human acceptance authority and the gate on future automated S.N.O.W. proposals.
---

Accepted S.N.O.W. classifications require an explicit action by a named staff member. An unattended API key must not acquire this authority merely because legacy authorization grants it a supervisor role.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requires model proposals and their metadata to remain separate, and only staff actions may set or override the accepted value. A proposal arriving later must never replace an existing staff decision.

**How to apply:** Proposers may update only proposal data. Preserve the accepted tier and its decision history, and invalidate stale staff decisions when the proposal changes. The owner has authorized deterministic rules-v1 and rules-v2, not an AI integration or model call. Preserve prior proposal versions and historical backtest inputs when revising rules.

The owner defines SURPLUS as supply relative to demand, not long shelf life. Item-level food rules must never propose SURPLUS; it remains a staff judgment until stock-versus-demand data exists. Missing metadata is not proof that physical food is unlabeled or undated; unknown facts must cause abstention unless another explicit rule applies. The owner explicitly added freshly prepared perishable catering/restaurant/kitchen food without label/date evidence as URGENT, same-day distribution only; do not broaden this exception to generic groceries or all missing expiry fields.

**Why:** These are the owner's written food rules and the reason a sealed, dated item can disagree with a historical Notion SURPLUS decision without either value being silently changed.

An explicitly authorized historical Notion import may initialize an empty accepted layer from a staff decision already recorded in Notion, with source “Notion import.” This is a provenance-import exception, not permission for a rule or model to accept proposals. Reruns must never replace local acceptance or invent an original staff author.
