name: Notion identity imports
description: Why Notion history imports preserve both ID lineages without manufacturing warehouse intake.
---

Preserve `DS-` and `DS-L-` as separate ID lineages, including exact formatting. Missing source IDs must remain missing; external IDs remain external and require review. A collision is not authority to merge two records. Retain every known source ID as an alias and the Notion page as provenance.

**Why:** The owner explicitly forbids renumbering, merging, or inventing IDs. Historical Notion records also include services and financial contributions, and some lack T.I.E.R. classifications; identity import is not authority to turn these into physical inventory or default missing classifications to Resources.

**How to apply:** Import identity/snapshot records independently of complete intake records. Any later promotion into inventory requires explicit staff curation and must reuse the durable identity, not create another item with the same display ID. Keep canonical DS allocation serialized with imports, reserve all canonical aliases, and never let DS-L numbers advance the DS sequence.
