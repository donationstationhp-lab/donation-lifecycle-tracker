---
name: Community ownership links
description: Rules for private community history and staff-mediated record ownership.
---

Community history must be authorized by the signed-in Clerk identity and resolved only through explicit staff-created ownership links. Tracking codes, names, phone numbers, and email addresses are not ownership proof.

**Why:** Public references are enumerable and contact fields can collide; inferring ownership would expose another person's claims, appointments, or service history.

**How to apply:** Keep the ownership table one-owner-per-record, allow staff-only link and unlink actions, derive related records only from already-owned IDs, and return an allowlisted personal-safe projection rather than raw operational rows.
