Refine Donation Station into the W.O.W. Universal Servicing system.

Core language:
W.O.W. = War On Waste / We All Stop Trashing Earth.

Doctrine:
Receive what is.
Gain what it means.
Give what completes.

Expanded sequence:
Receive → Gain → Give → Build → Focus → Create → Master Build → Construct Bridging → Form Relationships → Universally Service

Interpretation:
Receiving = intake of donations, requests, offers, claims, appointments, volunteers, and partner opportunities.
Gaining = classification, verification, quality control, matching, reservations, scheduling, and due diligence.
Giving = distribution, completed appointments, fulfilled claims, acknowledgments, public tracking, and impact reporting.
Bridging = staff calendar, public schedule, routes, reservations, barter handoffs, partner coordination.
Relationships = donor, receiver, volunteer, partner, staff, station, community.
Universal servicing = the completed operating system that protects dignity and proves service happened.

Do not work on Twilio/SMS/OTP yet.

Refinements:
1. Add W.O.W. language to dashboard:
   - W.O.W. Operating System
   - Receive. Gain. Give.
   - War On Waste / We All Stop Trashing Earth
   - Receive the resource. Gain the truth. Give with proof.

2. Organize dashboard sections by:
   - Receiving
   - Gaining
   - Giving
   - Bridging
   - Relationships
   - Universal Service

3. Keep existing navigation, but add meaning:
   - Items = Received Resources
   - Claims = Service Requests / Gaining Verification
   - Calendar = Bridging Time
   - Routes = Bridging Movement
   - Transfers = Giving / Distribution
   - Review = Due Diligence
   - Tracking = Public Proof

4. Public tracking page:
   Keep it privacy-safe.
   Add gentle language:
   - This tracking page shows public-safe service progress.
   - Exact times remain locked to protect identities.
   - Donation Station receives, verifies, schedules, and gives with proof.

5. Staff calendar:
   Frame as the Bridging Calendar:
   - pickup/drop-off
   - receiver pickup
   - volunteer shift
   - market window
   - reserved item pickup
   - barter handoff later

6. Service activity timeline:
   Use public-safe labels:
   - Request Received

   - Classified

   - Matched
   - Scheduled
   - Ready for Pickup
   - Distributed

7. Privacy:
   Public pages must never expose:
   - names
   - phone numbers
   - emails
   - addresses
   - donor identity

   - private notes
   - internal staff notes

8. Outcome:
   The app should feel like one living system:
   Receive → Gain → Give
   with scheduling, tracking, barter, claims, and appointments all serving one W.O.W. operating body.
